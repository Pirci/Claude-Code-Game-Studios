# Steppeborn: Path of the Sky Wolf — AI Görsel Üretim Prompt'ları

*Türkçe alt-başlık: Gök Kurt'un Yolu*

Platform: **Gemini Nano Banana** (Nano Banana 2 / Nano Banana Pro)
Stil referansı: Türk-bozkır kültürel estetiği + sulu boya el çizimi + **kozmik
mitolojik katman** (Oğuz Kağan Destanı).

Prompt formatı: Nano Banana'nın önerdiği yapısal akış kullanılır:
`[Konu] + [Eylem/Sahne] + [Ortam/Bağlam] + [Kompozisyon] + [Stil/Ortam]`

Sahneyi **betimle**, anahtar kelime **listeleme**.

> **ÖNEMLİ — Konsept revizyonu (2026-07-14)**: Oyun artık Oğuz Kağan Destanı
> omurgalı. Aşağıdaki prompt'ların sulu boya tabanı ve bozkır kültürü geçerli,
> ama iki yeni katman eklendi:
> - **Kök Böri (gök kurt)**: Bozkurt motifi artık ilahi rehber — gökten inen
>   ışıkla çevrili, altın gözlü, yarı-saydam. "Sky Wolf / Kök Böri, celestial
>   guide wolf haloed in heavenly light."
> - **Ülgen / Erlik kozmik ikiliği**: Ülgen = parlak göksel altın-indigo ışık
>   (düzen). Erlik = mor-siyah çürüme, bozulmuş toprak, kötücül ruhlar (körmös).
>   Fethedilecek bölgeler ile *bozulmuş* bölgeler görsel olarak ayrışmalı.
>
> Eski "Ergenekon" ve "Kırgız Kağan" merkezli prompt'lar (1, 6) Oğuz temasına
> göre yeniden çerçevelenmeli — aşağıda 9. ve 10. maddede yeni prompt'lar var.

## 1. Ana Kampanya Haritası (Bölüm 1 — Ergenekon)

```
Generate an image of a top-down strategy game campaign map depicting
Ergenekon — a sacred mountain valley surrounded by massive rocky peaks
with a single narrow passage glowing with forge-light. Inside the
valley, circular Kyrgyz yurt camps with white felt walls and wooden
lattice frames are clustered beside a rushing mountain river. Herds
of stocky Mongol horses graze on alpine meadow patches. At the center
of the valley, a large open-air forge billows orange smoke as
blacksmiths work iron. Small hand-painted cavalry icons mark army
positions across the map. Dotted watercolor lines define region
borders between territories. The entire map is rendered in loose,
expressive watercolor technique — visible wet-on-wet bleeds, soft
pigment edges, granulating washes on the mountains, and deliberate
brushstroke texture throughout. Mountains are painted in layered
indigo and grey washes with dry-brush texture for rock faces. The
river flows with translucent cerulean and turquoise washes. Grass
areas use varied olive and sage green with visible brush marks. An
ornate border incorporates Kyrgyz shyrdak felt carpet patterns —
geometric ram horns, diamond chains, and curved tumар motifs — all
painted in watercolor with slightly bleeding edges. A compass rose
in the corner features a Mongol-Turkic tamga at its center. The
color palette feels like a hand-held travel sketchbook: earthy warm
tones, natural pigment colors, with splashes of vibrant felt-carpet
reds and deep indigo blue.
16:9 aspect ratio, 4K resolution, watercolor painting on textured
cold-press paper, hand-drawn feel, visible brushstrokes, artistic
imperfections.
```

## 2. Bozkır Kampanya Haritası (Bölüm 2 — İlk Göç)

```
Generate an image of a vast Central Asian steppe campaign map for a
strategy game, painted entirely in watercolor. The landscape stretches
wide and open — endless rolling grasslands under an enormous sky that
takes up the upper third of the composition. A caravan trail winds
from left to right, marked by loose watercolor dots and dashes. Along
the trail, clusters of Kyrgyz yurts with their distinctive curved
roof poles sit among horse herds. A grey steppe wolf — the sacred
Bozkurt — is painted in fluid ink-wash silhouette, standing on a
low ridge as a guide marker. Distant snow-capped Tian Shan mountains
line the horizon, painted in pale blue-violet washes fading into the
sky. The landscape transitions seasonally from left to right: lush
wet-on-wet green washes on the left for summer, dry-brush golden
ochre strokes in the center for autumn, and pale grey-white washes
with salt texture effects on the right as winter approaches. Wind is
suggested by long sweeping dry-brush strokes across the grass. Eagle
silhouettes — sacred to Kyrgyz hunting culture — soar in the sky.
The border features Kyrgyz ala-kiyiz felt appliqué patterns: stylized
eagles, mountain goat horns, and sun motifs, all painted in warm
watercolor. A compass rose incorporates a Kyrgyz tunduk symbol — the
circular crown of the yurt — at its center.
16:9 aspect ratio, 4K resolution, watercolor on rough textured paper,
visible wet-on-wet bleeds, granulating pigments, hand-painted feel.
```

## Nano Banana Kullanım İpuçları

- **Sahne betimle, anahtar kelime listeleme**: "watercolor, steppe, army" yerine sahneyi anlatın
- **Sulu boya terimleri kullan**: "wet-on-wet bleed", "dry-brush texture", "granulating wash", "cold-press paper", "pigment bloom" gibi terimler Nano Banana'da iyi sonuç verir
- **Konuşarak iyileştir**: İlk sonucu aldıktan sonra "add more visible brushstrokes", "make the wolf fur more fluid", "increase the golden light intensity" gibi takip prompt'ları kullanın
- **Kültürel detay ekleyin**: "Kyrgyz shyrdak pattern", "Mongol deel robe", "tunduk crown ring", "composite bow" gibi spesifik terimler doğru kültürel referansları tetikler
- **Metin istiyorsanız**: Önce metin konseptini ayrı bir turda üretin, sonra görsele entegre edin
- **En boy oranı**: 16:9 (haritalar, menü), 9:16 (cutscene), 3:4 (rapor), 1:1 (ikonlar), 3:1 (sprite sheet)
- **Model seçimi**: Detaylı sahneler için Nano Banana Pro, hızlı iterasyon için Nano Banana 2
